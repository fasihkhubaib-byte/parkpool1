ParkPool

Live parking with shade for IBA students, and a carpool board that works like a bus timetable.

  1. Drag the time slider under the map and watch shade and lighting move across campus.
  2. Tap a green spot, then choose Guide me or I parked here.
  3. Offer a ride while parked: your spot shows when it will be free.
  4. Book a seat on the Rides board and get a boarding pass.
  5. Check the Shuttle tab for the next Main ↔ City Campus bus, then ask a question in the IBA Hub.

Camera detection, payments and email codes are simulated. Your data stays in this browser.
